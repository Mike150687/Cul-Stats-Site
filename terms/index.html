Terms of Service

1. Who we are

Cul Stats is a GAA match-analytics app at culstats.ie, provided by Michael Webb trading as Cul Stats ("Cul Stats", "we", "us"). Contact: admin@culstats.ie. These terms are a contract between us and the club that takes out a licence ("the Club"). By using the app, every person who records for the Club also agrees to them.

2. Words used here

  • Analyst: a person the Club authorises to record and review matches, with their own login.
  • Club Admin: an Analyst who may also manage the Club's other Analysts.
  • Viewer: a manager, coach or player given read-only access without a login, with a season code (Team Access) or, for one match, a match analysis code (section 5).
  • Club Data: everything the Club's Analysts put into the app: matches, events, squads, player names, crests, notes, targets and settings.
  • The Owner: the person operating Cul Stats for us, who administers licences and has the rights in section 10.

3. The licence

We grant the Club a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use Cul Stats for its own teams for the licence period, for the number of Analyst logins set out in the Club's plan. Viewers are not limited. The licence belongs to the Club, not to any individual: when an Analyst leaves the Club, the Club keeps its data and may invite a replacement.

4. Accounts and invites

  • Each Analyst signs in with their own email address and password and joins the Club with an invite code made for that address. Logins must not be shared.
  • The Club is responsible for who it asks us to invite, and must tell us promptly when someone should be removed.
  • Analysts must keep their passwords secure and tell us at admin@culstats.ie if they suspect misuse.
  • Season codes give read-only access to anyone who holds them. The Club decides who receives one, and can replace or withdraw a code at any time.

5. Fees, renewal and ending the licence

  • The Club pays the fee for its plan, per 12-month licence period: Team (1 team, 2 Analyst logins) €249; Club (up to 4 teams, 5 Analyst logins) €599; Whole Club (every team, 12 Analyst logins) €999. Each extra Analyst login is €75 per licence period. A new Club may first have a free trial of 14 days with every feature; nothing is charged for it, and the trial simply ends unless the Club chooses a plan. Fees are paid in advance for each licence period, either online by card when the Club buys its plan or within 30 days of an invoice, and are not refundable for a part-used period, except where the law requires.
  • Match analysis is a separate, one-off service: €99 per match, charged when the Club asks for it, with or without a licence. What the Club gets: we record every action in the match, for both teams, from a video the Club supplies, and give the Club a code with view-only access to that match in Cul Stats: its reports, maps and log, with a link to each moment in the video. When: within 48 hours of the video reaching us in a form we can watch; that clock starts then, not when the Club pays. If the video cannot be used, we ask for another. Starting straight away: by paying, the Club asks us to start as soon as the video arrives. It may withdraw within 14 days; if it does so before the analysis is finished we refund the fee less a fair amount for the work already done, and once the analysis has been delivered in full that right ends. If anything is wrong, or the Club wants to cancel, it emails admin@culstats.ie and we confirm any refund by email. Whose it is: the Club owns the match data and its video. We hold them only to carry out the analysis and to give the Club access to it, make no claim to either, and use nothing from them except anonymised, aggregated figures that cannot identify the Club or any player. So that the clips can be watched, we put the video on YouTube as an unlisted video: it cannot be found by searching, only opened from its link, which is in the match. The code works, and we keep the match and the video, until the Club asks us to delete them; we then delete both within 30 days. The Club can ask for a copy of the match (CSV or PDF reports) at any time. The video: the Club confirms it has the right to give us the video and to have it analysed, including the consent of anyone needed, such as the venue, players, and the parents or guardians of players under 18, and it remains responsible for the footage under data-protection law, with us acting on its behalf.
  • The licence renews at the end of each period unless either side gives 30 days' notice in writing (email to admin@culstats.ie is enough).
  • If fees are unpaid 14 days after they fall due, or the Club seriously breaks these terms, we may suspend the licence. While suspended, Analysts cannot record or save new matches, but the Club's existing data is kept and stays readable. Access returns in full once the issue is resolved.
  • When the licence ends, the Club has 90 days to download its data (Matches → Download everything). After that we delete the Club Data from our systems, apart from anonymised, aggregated figures (section 10) and anything the law requires us to keep.

6. Acceptable use

The Club and its Analysts must not:

  • use Cul Stats for anything unlawful, or to record or publish anything defamatory or abusive about a player, official or club;
  • try to get into another club's data, test or break our security, or get around the licence;
  • copy, resell or rebuild the app, or use it to build a competing product;
  • put in personal data beyond what match analysis needs (for example, no medical details, contact details or addresses of players).

7. The Club's responsibilities for player data

The Club decides whose matches are recorded and what is recorded about players, so under data-protection law the Club is the controller of its players' personal data. The Club must:

  • have a lawful basis for recording players' names and performance; for most clubs, the legitimate interest of coaching and selecting teams;
  • tell its players, and the parents or guardians of players under 18, that matches are recorded and analysed with Cul Stats, what is recorded, who can see it (including us, as section 10 explains), and how to ask about it; our Privacy Policy below can be shared for this;
  • take extra care with players under 18, following GAA guidance and its own child safeguarding policy;
  • pass on to us promptly any request it receives from a player that needs our help to answer.

Opposition players are recorded too. The Club must use what it records about other teams only for its own match preparation and analysis.

8. Who owns the data

The Club owns its Club Data. We hold it to provide the service, and the Club can download all of it at any time. No other club can see the Club's data. A Viewer sees only what the Club shares with a code.

9. Availability and backups

We aim to keep Cul Stats available at all times, but it depends on the internet and on third-party services (section 4 of the Privacy Policy), and we do not promise it will never be interrupted. The app saves each match on the recording device first, so recording continues without a connection. We back Club Data up to the cloud, but the Club should also keep its own copy using Download everything. We may update the app at any time; an update never interrupts a match being recorded.

10. How we use Club Data, and anonymised figures

We use Club Data only to provide Cul Stats to the Club: to run, support, fix and secure the service for it. The Owner opens a club's data only for those reasons: when the Club asks for help, or when it is needed to keep the service working and safe. We may also use anonymised, aggregated figures drawn from all clubs' data (for example, the average number of shots in a match, or how often a kickout of a given length is kept) to improve Cul Stats, including its expected-points model and benchmarks. Those figures cannot identify any club, team or player. We never use one club's data in another club's analysis, including the analysis the Owner does for their own club, and we never sell Club Data or give it to anyone else.

11. Our intellectual property

The app, its design, its models, methods and reports belong to us. The Club may use the reports it produces from its own data however it likes, including sharing them with its players and members.

12. Liability

Cul Stats is a tool to help coaching decisions; the Club remains responsible for the decisions it makes. Figures depend on what is recorded and may contain errors. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, and our total liability to the Club in any 12 months is limited to the fees the Club paid us in the 12 months before the claim. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

13. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Ireland, and the Irish courts have jurisdiction.

14. Changes to these terms

We may change these terms. We will email the Club's Admins at least 30 days before a change that reduces the Club's rights takes effect, and the Club may end the licence before then for a refund of any unused fees.

3. Who can see it

  • The club's own Analysts see everything their club records.
  • People the club gives a season code, usually managers, coaches and sometimes players, see the club's season reports, including every player's record, read only.
  • People holding a live-match code see that match as it is recorded. The live copy is deleted shortly after the session ends.
  • People holding a reel link see that reel: its clips, played from the club's own unlisted YouTube videos, and their captions, which may name a player.
  • The Cul Stats Owner opens a club's data only to provide or support the service, never for any other club's analysis, including the club the Owner also analyses for.
  • Other clubs cannot see your club's data. A player recorded by two clubs appears in each club's data separately.
  • We do not give personal data to anyone else unless the law requires it.

4. Where it is held

In the club's cloud account with Google Firebase in the EU (Belgium), and on the devices of the club's Analysts. Sign-in, the app, the information website at www.culstats.ie and the typeface are delivered through services that may process IP addresses outside the EU (Google, GitHub, Netlify), and a reel from a YouTube video plays in YouTube's own player (Google); those transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses. The full list of services is in section 15 of the Terms of Service. If an Analyst uses the optional AI analyst, match figures go from their device to Anthropic under the Analyst's own account.

6. Security

Data travels encrypted. Each club's data is walled off from every other club's by database rules that are tested before any change, and only the club's Analysts and the Owner can read it.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see the data held about you, have it corrected, have it deleted, restrict or object to its use, or receive a copy. If you are a player, ask your club first, since it decides what is recorded about you, and we will help it answer. You can also write to us at admin@culstats.ie. We reply within one month.

8. Children

Many players are under 18. Clubs record only match information about them, never contact details or sensitive information, and must tell players and their parents or guardians how Cul Stats is used. A parent or guardian can make any of the requests in section 7 for their child.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, contact us first at admin@culstats.ie. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission, 6 Pembroke Row, Dublin 2, D02 X963 (dataprotection.ie).

10. Changes

We will update this policy when what we collect or how we use it changes, and show the date at the top.
