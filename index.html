Live tagging

Tap, tap, back to watching the game.

Pick the action, the player and where it happened on the pitch. The time is taken at your first tap, not when you finish, so the clock is right even when the play is quick.

  • After a score or a wide, Kickout already knows who is kicking out
  • Every action is said back to you for two seconds, so a wrong name is one tap from fixed
  • Undo names what it is about to undo

Questions

What clubs ask first.

What if there's no signal at the pitch?

It keeps recording. Everything is saved on the device as you go and backs up when the signal comes back.

Is it for hurling or camogie?

Not yet — Cúl Stats is built for Gaelic football.

Who can see our data?

Only people your club gives access to. Analysts log in; managers and players view with your club's Team Access code. Your opposition cannot see it.

Do we have to upload our match video?

No. Paste the link to your Veo, YouTube or OneDrive video, or pick the file on your laptop. The video stays where it is.

What happens when the trial ends?

Pick a plan and carry on — every match you recorded is kept. If you don't, the trial simply stops; nothing is charged, because nothing is billed until you choose a plan.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Every match exports to CSV and Excel, every report to PDF, and there's a Download everything file for the whole club.
