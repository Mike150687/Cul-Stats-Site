Privacy Policy

Cul Stats Last updated: October 2026

Cul Stats ("we", "us", "our") provides Gaelic football match-analytics software and tools at culstats.ie and via our companion mobile applications. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Irish data protection laws.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and your rights regarding your information. For any questions or data requests, you can contact us at admin@culstats.ie.

1. Information We Collect

To provide match analysis and club administration features, we collect and process the following categories of data:

  • Account Credentials: When an analyst or club administrator creates an account, we collect an email address and securely authenticate via Firebase Authentication. We do not store plain-text passwords.
  • Match & Performance Data: Information entered into the app during match tagging, including team names, player jersey numbers, match events (shots, kickouts, turnovers, scores), timestamps, pitch zones, and tactical notes.
  • Club Configuration: Club names, team lists, panel metadata, and user role assignments (e.g., admin or recorder status) required to manage club access.
  • Device & Usage Diagnostics: Standard technical logs, local application state caching (via IndexedDB), and referrer data required to maintain secure connectivity and app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the operation and performance of the Cul Stats service:

  • To authenticate users and secure access to team and club data dashboards.
  • To calculate match performance metrics, expected points ($xP$), and pitch analytics.
  • To synchronize live match events between authorized devices during game-day recording.
  • To provide technical support, service updates, and account administration notices.

3. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored securely using Google Firebase infrastructure (Firestore and Realtime Database) located within European server regions, complying with high industry security standards. Local offline caching is stored securely on your device using browser IndexedDB storage.

4. Third-Party Services

We minimize third-party data sharing. Where external tools are utilized to support the application, they operate under strict privacy frameworks:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure cloud authentication and real-time database synchronization.
  • Video Platforms (YouTube / OneDrive / Veo): If you link match video URLs, playback occurs within standard container frames or platform players governed by their respective platform terms.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Match records and club data are retained for as long as your club licence is active, allowing historical season-to-season analysis. You may request the complete deletion of your club account, user credentials, and associated database records at any time by emailing admin@culstats.ie.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

Under European data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. Because clubs act as data controllers for their match logs and player rosters, club administrators manage team-level data directly within the application dashboard.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any material changes will be communicated via our website or app notification channels.
